Python · Machine learning · Bayesian reasoning · Team project

Guardian Agent — Personalized Weather-Hazard Decision Support

An LSU Artificial Intelligence team project integrating live weather APIs, a gradient-boosted classifier, Bayesian risk reasoning, rule-based recommendations, and a Streamlit interface.

  • Team/repository-reported ROC-AUC of 0.759 on 21,281 held-out county-day cells.
  • Automated coverage across data, modeling, planning, output, and interface components.
  • Contributed to project implementation; Maby Gavilan Abanto reviewed the project and prepared the presentation.

Python · Streamlit · Computational macroeconomics

Fiscal Policy in an Overlapping-Generations Model

An interactive implementation of a two-period overlapping-generations model with capital and labor taxation, transfers, population growth, and technology growth.

  • Numerical steady-state solutions and transition-path simulations.
  • Comparisons across fiscal-policy and growth scenarios.
  • Interactive visualizations and downloadable CSV/Excel result tables.

Publications

Peer-reviewed work

Study-level findings are described separately from individual contributions.

Using Deep Learning to Enhance Business Intelligence in Organizational Management

Sina Gholami, Erfan Zarafshan, Reza Sheikh, and Shib Sankar Sana · Data Science in Finance and Economics 3(4), 337–353 · 2023

The study evaluates a convolutional neural network for classifying unstructured customer-feedback text and reports 88% accuracy and an F-score of 0.86.

Resource Planning System for Organisations: A Soft Computing Approach

Erfan Zarafshan, Sina Gholami, Reza Sheikh, and Shib Sankar Sana · International Journal of Services, Economics and Management 12(3), 272–293 · 2021

The study applies rough set theory to data from 154 ERP users and examines implementation cost, vendor, software quality, and functional capability.

The Analysis and Interpretation of Quality Cost Using Rough Set Theory: A Case Study from the Ceramic Industry

Erfan Zarafshan, Samira Rezaei Toroghi, Reza Sheikh, and Shib Sankar Sana · International Journal of Service and Computing Oriented Manufacturing 4(1), 11–31 · 2021

The study analyzes five years of quality-cost information from 11 ceramic-industry firms and derives interpretable decision rules using rough set theory.
